01 / About

Aerospace engineering,
controls, and simulation.

I’m an Aerospace Engineering student at the University of Florida with a focus on guidance, navigation, and control, spacecraft dynamics, and simulation.

My experience includes spacecraft rendezvous and capture research, rocket flight simulation, and flight-control development for a VTOL vehicle. Across these projects, I’ve worked with dynamic modeling, control-system design, numerical simulation, and validation against established software and physical testing.

I’m currently interested in opportunities involving spacecraft GNC, flight controls, autonomous systems, and modeling and simulation.

01

ONGOING RESEARCH · UNIVERSITY OF FLORIDA · 2026–PRESENT

Autonomous Tether-Assisted Capture

Developing MATLAB simulations to study post-capture despin and stabilization of a non-cooperative resident space object using controlled tether forces. My work focuses on flexible tether dynamics, contact geometry, orbital relative-motion effects, and the tradeoff between despin performance and translational energy.

Collaborative research under Dr. Norman Fitz-Coy alongside Bogochan Ondes.

02

FLORIDA ROCKET LAB · FLIGHT SIMULATION

Rocket Flight Simulation

Worked on a Python-based 3DOF flight-dynamics simulator for a liquid-propellant competition rocket, incorporating aerodynamic forces, atmospheric effects, and Monte Carlo analysis. Validated predicted trajectories against OpenRocket, RASAero II, and flight data, achieving results within approximately 5% of measured flight performance.

  • Python
  • 3DOF
  • Aerodynamics
  • Monte Carlo
03

FLIGHT CONTROLS · HARDWARE TESTING

Skipper — Mono-Propelled VTOL Flight Controls

As part of Florida Rocket Lab’s GNC team, contributed to the development and testing of the flight-control system for a mono-propelled VTOL vehicle. My work focused on actuator implementation and control integration in MATLAB/Simulink, alongside simulation and physical flight testing.

  • Simulink
  • PID / LQI
  • Dynamics
  • Flight Test
